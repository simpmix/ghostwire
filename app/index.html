◈ QUICK HELP

  
WHAT IS THIS? — A private messenger. Messages leave your device as unreadable noise and only your chosen peer can open them.

  
FIRST STEPS — 1) Forge an identity. 2) Find a friend's username and request DMs. 3) When they accept, chat. Or press TRANSMIT with no peer to test alone.

  
THE 16-CHAR KEY — Your master secret. It seals your archives. Write it down; nobody can recover it for you.

  
.BIN FILES — The chat itself, saved on your computer. LOAD CHAT brings it back anytime.

  
BURN — Messages that self-destruct seconds after being read.

  
NEARBY — Link to peers on your local network with zero internet.

  
SHORTCUTS — Ctrl+K find peer · Ctrl+T wire tap · Ctrl+B burn · Ctrl+L lock · Ctrl+P palette · Ctrl+Shift+T cycle skin · Ctrl+Shift+Del panic.
